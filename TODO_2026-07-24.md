## ⚠️ Critical Issue — Mobile Navigation Is Completely Broken

The `Navbar` has no mobile menu. All navigation links and the "Book a Call" CTA are
wrapped in `hidden md:flex` and there is only a comment in their place:

```tsx
{/* Mobile menu button could go here */}
```

On any screen narrower than 768px, the site has **zero navigation**. This is the
single highest-priority fix. No other polish work should ship before this is resolved.

**Scope of fix:** Build a new `MobileNav` client component with a hamburger toggle
button, a slide-in or dropdown drawer, all navigation links from `navigationLinks`,
the Login link, and the "Book a Call" CTA. The hamburger button should be visible
at `md:hidden` and the drawer should trap focus and close on outside click or `Esc`.

## 🔴 Priority 1 — Structural Fixes (Blocks All Pages)

### 1.1 — Courses Page Has Nested `<main>`
**File:** `src/app/courses/page.tsx`  
**Issue:** The page wraps content in `<main className="pt-24 ...">`. The root
`layout.tsx` already provides the `<main id="main-content">` landmark.
This creates a nested `<main>`, which is invalid HTML and fails WCAG 1.3.1.  
**Fix:** Replace `<main ...>` with `<div ...>` or a fragment `<>`.

## 🟠 Priority 2 — Mobile Breakpoint Issues

### 2.1 — Hero Buttons May Overflow on Small Phones
**File:** `src/app/page.tsx` — Hero section  
**Issue:** Both hero CTAs have `min-w-70 whitespace-nowrap`. On 320px devices
(iPhone SE), `min-w-70` (280px) combined with `px-4` container padding leaves
almost no room. The `flex-col items-start` layout stacks them, but each button
could overflow the container edge.  
**Fix:** Remove `min-w-70` and let buttons size naturally, or cap at `min-w-[240px]`
and verify on a 360px viewport.

### 2.2 — Homepage Hero H1 Is Very Large on Mobile
**File:** `src/app/page.tsx`  
**Issue:** `text-6xl` (60px) on mobile. Combined with `leading-[1.05]`, the two-line
heading "Confidence is / Built Here." renders extremely large on phones. No `sm:`
intermediate step exists between base and `md:text-8xl`.  
**Fix:** Add `sm:text-7xl` as a step, or lower the base to `text-5xl sm:text-6xl md:text-8xl`.

### 2.3 — Pathway Cards Have No Padding Reduction on Mobile
**File:** `src/app/page.tsx` — Pathway overview section  
**Issue:** The Legal and Credit pathway cards use `p-12` (48px) with no mobile
reduction. On a 375px phone, this leaves a very narrow content column inside the card.  
**Fix:** Change to `p-8 md:p-12` or `p-6 sm:p-8 md:p-12`.

### 2.4 — Legal & Credit Hero Stats Grid Missing on Tablet
**Files:** `src/components/legal/LegalHero.tsx`, `src/components/credit/CreditHero.tsx`  
**Issue:** The right-column stats grid is `hidden lg:block`. Tablet users (768px–1023px)
see only the left text column with no visual interest on the right, and the
`lg:grid-cols-2` layout never activates for them either — they get a single-column
layout the same as mobile.  
**Fix:** Consider showing a condensed 2×2 stats grid at `md:` or at minimum allowing
the grid to activate at `md:grid-cols-2` for the two-column hero layout.

### 2.5 — Hero Section Padding Pattern Inconsistent
**Files:** `LegalHero.tsx`, `CreditHero.tsx`  
**Issue:** Both heroes use `px-6 sm:px-12` directly on the `<section>` element
rather than the project-standard `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8` container.
This creates inconsistent side gutters, particularly between 640px–1024px (tablet),
where `sm:px-12` (48px) is much wider than the rest of the site's `sm:px-6` (24px).  
**Fix:** Wrap inner content in a `<div className="max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8">` container matching the rest of the site, and remove the `px-` from the `<section>`.

### 2.6 — Footer Jumps From 1 Column to 4 Columns at `md`
**File:** `src/components/layout/Footer.tsx`  
**Issue:** `grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-4`. At exactly 768px (the `md` breakpoint) the
footer switches from stacked to a 4-column layout. On most tablets this is very
cramped — the Logo/About column is `col-span-2`, leaving two narrow link columns.  
**Fix:** Add `sm:grid-cols-2` so the footer transitions through a 2-column layout on
tablets before expanding to 4 on larger screens. The logo block should span 2 columns
at `sm:` as well.

### 2.7 — ScopeSection Card Padding Has No Mobile Reduction
**File:** `src/components/legal/ScopeSection.tsx`  
**Issue:** The two-panel card uses `p-12 lg:p-16`. On mobile (1 column), `p-12` (48px)
on all four sides is tight for a content-heavy list.  
**Fix:** Change to `p-8 sm:p-10 lg:p-16`.

### 2.8 — `skew-y-3` Image in AboutVeeSection Has No Mobile Consideration
**File:** `src/components/common/AboutVeeSection.tsx`  
**Issue:** The decorative `skew-y-3` transform on the image frame and its companion
`-skew-y-3` inner correction look fine on desktop but the absolute-positioned
decorative border frame (`translate-x-4 translate-y-4`) can extend outside the
overflow boundary on narrow screens.  
**Fix:** Add `overflow-hidden` to the outer container or confirm this is visually
acceptable by checking on 375px viewport. Low priority — visual only.

## 🟡 Priority 3 — Content & Architecture Issues

### 3.1 — Featured Offers on Homepage Are Hardcoded
**File:** `src/app/page.tsx` — Featured offers section  
**Issue:** The three featured offer cards (Debt Defense Mastery™, Credit Mastery
Blueprint™, Ultimate Empowerment Bundle™) have their names, prices, descriptions,
and CTA labels written directly as inline data in `page.tsx`. This violates the
project rule that all offer data must come from `src/content/offers.ts`.  
**Fix:** Import offers from `src/content/offers.ts`, filter for `featured: true`,
and render using `<OfferCard>`.

### 3.2 — Inline Disclaimer in Homepage ✅ Decision Made
**File:** `src/app/page.tsx`  
**Decision:** Keep as intentional reinforcement. The inline "not a law firm" notice
between the hero and pathway overview section is a deliberate second touchpoint,
separate from the global `DisclaimerBanner` in the layout. No action needed.
